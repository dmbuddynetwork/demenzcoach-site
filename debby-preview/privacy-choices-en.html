Privacy Choices

Demenz Coach works without a user account. You can optionally use an account for cross-device backup.

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Delete local data

  1. Open “For me” in Demenz Coach.
  2. Select “Settings”, then “Privacy & Data”.
  3. Confirm “Delete all local data”.

This removes onboarding, learning progress, check-ins, history, consent settings and saved phrases from that device. Uninstalling the app also deletes its local app data.

Manage your account, cloud data and export

Under “Settings → Account & Backup”, you can sign in with Apple or a telephone number, export your cloud data, sign out, or select “Delete account and cloud data”. Before permanent deletion, you must confirm your identity again with Apple or an SMS code. Deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account and all associated synchronised records; it does not cancel or refund an App Store subscription.

Signing out alone does not delete data. Local guest data and local data for signed-in accounts remain separate.

Manage optional AI functions

Demenz Coach uses AI for personal text assistance; there is no separate consent switch for that core service. Voluntary consents for voice input, translation, Biography context and automated feedback analysis can each be withdrawn for future processing under “Settings → Privacy & Data”.

Manage internal quality review

The separate option for OpenAI project logs is off by default and can be changed independently of personal AI assistance under “Settings → Privacy & Data”. Disabling it prevents new response objects from being stored. Previously stored entries expire within 30 days. For access or an earlier deletion request, use the contact address below.

Manage analytics and crash diagnostics

Optional app analytics is disabled by default and can be enabled or disabled in the app settings. Anonymous crash diagnostics can also be disabled there for future reports. Website analytics loads only after “Allow analytics” is selected. Use “Privacy settings” at the bottom of any website page to change or withdraw that choice. Withdrawal applies to future analytics events.

Revoke microphone access

You can disable microphone access at any time in iOS Settings under “Privacy & Security → Microphone”. Text input and local content remain available.

Manage your subscription

Apple manages subscriptions. Use Apple Subscriptions to view status or cancel. Deleting local data or the app does not cancel a subscription.

Exercise privacy rights

For access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection or other questions, email dementica@minini.de. Do not send health data. Because we cannot access data held only locally, it can be deleted only on the relevant device.

See the Privacy Policy for full details.
