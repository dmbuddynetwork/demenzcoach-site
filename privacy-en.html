Privacy Policy

This policy provides the information required by Articles 13 and 14 GDPR about the processing of personal data in the Demenz Coach iOS app and on this website. The app can be used without an account; an account is only needed for optional backup and for a web subscription.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

In short

  • You can use the app without an account. Most of what you type or save in the app stays on your device.
  • For a personal AI answer, the app sends your text through our server to the company OpenAI in the USA. This happens only after you have expressly agreed. You can take back your consent at any time. Without consent, there are no AI answers.
  • Please do not write names, addresses or medical records in the input fields. Describe instead what is happening (“Mom wants to go home”).
  • We are not a doctor and not a nursing advice service. Demenz Coach gives general ideas for conversations and daily life, no diagnosis and no treatment.
  • We do not sell data and do not use an advertising ID. Measurement and advertising only happen with your yes.
  • You can ask for information and have your data deleted at any time: debby@dementiacoachapp.com.

The full statement follows below. It is not a contract term; you do not have to agree to it. It only informs you.

2. Data on your device

Answers from the introduction, display options, learning progress, check-ins, recently used situations, companion profiles, saved sentences and remembered answers are stored by the app on your device using Apple technology (UserDefaults, SwiftData). We do not automatically receive this content.

This storage is necessary so that the app offers the functions you want to use. Where Section 25 TDDDG applies, this is Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG; for personal data, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR applies. The data stays until you delete it in the app (“Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete all my data”) or remove the app.

The app remembers frequent answers on your device (at most 40 answers, each for at most 14 days), so that the same question can be answered immediately and also without a network. The keys for this are checksums, not your text.

3. Voluntary account and backup

Demenz Coach works without an account. You need an account to back up your data across devices, take out a subscription on our website or use the additional free introductory allowance in the web app. The web app also offers Google sign-in. Firebase processes the Google account details you share (email address, profile name and picture) for authentication. This does not subscribe you to a newsletter. The web app uses an email sign-in link through Firebase Authentication (Google Ireland Limited), without a phone number or SMS. Firebase processes your email address, user ID and sign-in method. To associate a requested link safely, we temporarily keep the email address, random request identifier, existing user ID and return destination in functional browser storage. This request is deleted after successful confirmation or discarded on the next visit after 30 minutes. The confirmation code itself is not stored in browser storage. Signing in does not subscribe you to a newsletter; that requires separate, voluntary consent. In the native iOS app, “Sign in with Apple” and phone-number/SMS sign-in remain available. With Apple, depending on your choice, your name, email address or Apple relay address can be transmitted. With a phone number, Google also stores the number to prevent spam and abuse; your mobile provider may charge for SMS.

A second type of sign-in is only linked to the existing account after your express confirmation. Accounts are not merged automatically by email address or telephone number.

In the native app, after sign-in, we store companion profiles, everyday aids, experiences and favourites that you created voluntarily in Cloud Firestore (Firebase, region europe-west1, Belgium) and sync them between your signed-in devices. According to the access rules, each account can only access its own records. Please do not enter diagnoses, doctors’ letters or other health documents of a person in your care there either.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The cloud data stays until you delete individual content or the whole account. Under “Settings → Account and backup”, you can export your data, sign out and permanently delete the account together with the cloud data. Signing out deletes neither cloud data nor local data.

4. Personal AI help in the app

What happens. Demenz Coach is an AI-supported service. When you describe a situation or choose a prepared situation and tap “Get answer”, the app sends your text and technically necessary details (for example language, chosen goal, app version) to our server at Google Cloud (Firebase Functions, region europe-west1). From there it goes to the interface of OpenAI, which creates the answer. We currently use the language model GPT-6.1 Terra (“gpt-6.1-terra”). We can change the model; the same protective measures then apply. If, for a goal button (“What do you want to achieve?”), the app uses a text that it does not yet know, this text also goes to the AI (your consent applies to this too), so that the buttons are worded suitably.

Your express consent. When you send for the first time, the app asks in a separate window (“Before the AI answers”). It says there: Your entries, including details about health concerning you or the person you support, go to OpenAI in the USA so that your answer is created there. You agree with a separate tick box. Without consent, no texts go to the AI and there are no AI answers. Health data is especially protected; that is why the consent is express (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). It is voluntary. You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future under “Settings → Privacy”; withdrawing is as easy as agreeing. Until withdrawal, the processing remains lawful. As proof, we store the time and the wording of your consent on your device. If we change the wording significantly, the app asks you again.

Transfer to the USA. OpenAI processes the data in the USA. Where the transfer is not already covered by an adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses, it relies on your express consent (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR). The level of data protection in the USA can be lower than in the EU; for example, authorities there can access data under certain conditions.

What OpenAI does with the data. We send requests to the OpenAI Responses API with “store: false”. OpenAI does not use API inputs and answers to train its models unless we expressly allow it (we have not). Security and abuse logs that OpenAI keeps separately can be kept for up to 30 days under OpenAI’s rules. In our OpenAI account, we have switched off the sharing of data for training.

What we store. Our server keeps no permanent database of your situation descriptions, answers or voice recordings. So that the same request does not have to be paid for twice, the server keeps everyday answers to exactly the same text and the same goal in working memory for at most 14 days; they disappear at the latest with every restart. This never applies to answers with profile details, add-ons or safety answers. As operating statistics, the server counts per day how many requests and answers there were, without text, IP address or identifier.

Profile details. Details from a companion profile (for example preferences) and your tapped answers to fixed questions are only included if “Use profile for personal answers” is switched on. You see them before sending and can deselect them there. They then go to OpenAI with “store: false”.

Emergencies and fixed answers. The app first checks your text on your device for signs of danger (for example smell of gas, choking, fire). If it detects one, it shows a fixed answer pointing to the emergency number 112, without the text going to the AI.

Further functions with their own consent. For voice input (the recording is sent to OpenAI only to be converted into text and is not stored), translation of answers and biography context, the app asks for a separate consent before each transfer (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Each consent applies only to its purpose and can be withdrawn individually.

Quality sharing (off). Switched off by default and only found in the settings. If you switch it on, OpenAI stores new texts together with answers for up to 30 days in our OpenAI project log; authorised team members can view them for quality checks. Switching it off stops new storage; entries already stored expire after 30 days at the latest. The AI help works just the same without this sharing.

Notes on use. Please do not enter names, addresses, contact details, dates of birth, insurance numbers or documents with patient data. The app is not meant to process identifiable health data of third parties. Even if you describe in general terms what is close to a person, this is information about another person; share only as much as is necessary for the answer. Answers are created automatically by an AI system and are marked in the app as an AI suggestion. No decision with legal or similarly significant effect for you is made.

Not a medical device. Demenz Coach is a general communication and everyday aid. We do not recognise illnesses, do not give diagnoses, treatment or medication recommendations, and do not assess complaints.

5. Security, configuration and protection against abuse

Firebase Remote Config delivers technical settings to the app. Firebase App Check with Apple’s App Attest checks whether a request comes from a genuine app installation. In doing so, technical check data from your device is transmitted to Apple and Google. We process network data, above all the IP address, for a short time for operation, troubleshooting, security and request limiting; from this we form a pseudonymous security identifier. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is safe, stable and abuse-free operation.

Answers from our server are marked with “Cache-Control: no-store”. We keep security and system logs at Google Cloud only as long as is necessary for the purpose or because of legal duties (as a rule at most 30 days).

6. Measuring use and crash reports

Measuring use (Google Analytics for Firebase). Off until you agree. After your first good answer, the app asks once whether it may measure use (“Yes, gladly” / “No, thanks”). If you say “Yes”, Google processes technical events (for example whether the introduction, a request or a purchase worked, how far someone got in the introduction) as well as an app instance ID, device, app and interaction data. Texts, voice recordings, saved sentences and check-in content are never part of it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG. You can withdraw at any time under “Settings → Privacy”. We do not call this measurement anonymous, because it uses an app instance ID.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). In case of a crash or error, Crashlytics processes technical data: app version, device model, operating system, time, technical states and error traces. We set no user ID and send no texts. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (stable and safe app). You can switch off the reports in the settings; you then object for the future.

7. Purchases in the app, Apple Ads and Adapty

Apple handles purchases and subscriptions in the app. We do not receive full payment data. To display the offers and to check your subscription status, we use Adapty (Adapty Tech Inc.). In doing so, a random profile ID, the IP address, device and app details, interactions with offers, and purchase and subscription status can be processed. If you took out a subscription on our website, Adapty additionally receives your account ID and the subscription status, so that the subscription applies in the app.

If you open the app after an ad in Apple Ads, Adapty can process sparse attribution data through Apple’s AdServices interface (for example campaign, ad group, keyword, country, first or repeat download). We use this to assign installations, trial periods and subscriptions to our own ads. The Apple advertising ID (IDFA) stays off; we do not use the data for cross-app tracking or personalised advertising.

Legal bases: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (reliable provision, protection against abuse, sparing evaluation of our own advertising). You can object to the processing under point (f) (see section 16). Purchase and attribution data are stored according to the statutory retention periods and the rules of Apple and Adapty.

In doing so, Cloudflare processes technically necessary data such as IP address, time, requested file, referring page, and browser and device details. Cloudflare acts for us as a processor, in a worldwide network of data centres, usually close to the visitor. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the safe and fast delivery of the pages.

Privacy settings. On your first visit, a notice asks which measurement you allow. “Decline” and “Allow” look the same. Your browser stores your choice in local storage (key demenzcoach.analyticsConsent.v1). You can change it at any time through “Privacy settings” at the bottom of the page.

Comparing entries through /teller. This address randomly routes visits to the website or questionnaire. Only with your statistics consent do we remember the arm for 30 days: in local storage (demenzcoach.teller.v1, arm, expiry and milestones already counted) and a cookie (dc_teller_v1, only website or questionnaire, without a new individual identifier). Return visits keep the same arm during that period. After expiry, the local entry is removed or replaced on your next visit. With the same consent we count assignment, the first answered questionnaire step and questionnaire completion, and send the fixed labels teller_v1, a/b and website/questionnaire to our checkout. Without statistics consent we do not store or transmit this assignment; repeat visits may be routed differently and are excluded from the comparison. Withdrawing through “Privacy settings” removes the local entry and arm cookie and stops new measurement and new checkout attribution. This does not retroactively delete data already transmitted or legally required payment records; the other Google Analytics and Stripe information applies. Measurement covers only part of the visits and is not fully anonymous.

Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-5NRN1EMJDC). Only loaded after you have allowed “Statistics”. Before that, we send no analytics requests to Google. After that, pages and areas visited, approximate session length, referring page, browser, device and approximate location details, and clicks on key functions can be processed. We do not send text from input fields, care situations, health data or a user ID. Advertising storage, advertising user data, personalised advertising and Google Signals are switched off. Legal bases: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG. On withdrawal, the page tries to remove its Google cookies.

Support. If you write us an email, we process the sender, content and metadata in order to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, otherwise point (f)). We delete the messages as soon as we no longer need them and there is no retention duty. Please do not send any health data.

9. Demenz Translator on the website

Ready-to-use situations. Situations that you deliberately open are displayed in your browser. For text you enter yourself, AI first suggests suitable goals and then creates an answer after you choose your goal.

Saving sentences (on your device only). If you tap “Save” on an answer, your browser stores the sentence in local storage (key demenzcoach.savedPhrases.v1): the situation, the heading (for example “I want to go home.”) and the prepared answer sentence from our fixed answers. We never store your input, AI answers or names there. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. In the “My sentences” list on the home page you remove any sentence with “Remove”; otherwise the data stays until you delete it or clear your browser’s website data. This storage is a function you expressly asked for (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Finding helpful answers again. Choosing “Yes, save” stores up to 20 answers in your browser for your overview. For prepared answers, we keep the situation and goal and open the current version; for AI answers, we store the version shown to you. It may contain personal details. Anyone using the same browser can see it. Saving does not send answer content to us or anyone else. We do not store a separate copy of your input or sharing data as part of this collection. You can delete individual helpful answers or all of them. Clearing the website data also removes this collection. The separate “My sentences” list is unchanged.

Text size (on your device only). If you choose “A+” (larger text), your browser remembers this in local storage (key demenzcoach.textSize.v1, value “large”). This is the only setting, nothing is sent, and the value is deleted when you switch back. This is a display you expressly asked for (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Sharing. The share button next to an answer sentence opens WhatsApp or your phone’s share menu. Only the fixed answer sentence and the link to the sentence page are passed on, never your input or names. We do not learn anything about it. Whether and to whom you send the sentence is up to you; the privacy terms of the service you choose (for example WhatsApp) apply.

AI goals and answers. For goal suggestions, we send the situation you enter (at most 200 characters) and the chosen form of address (informal “du” or formal “Sie”) to our server at Google Cloud (Firebase, region europe-west1) and from there to the OpenAI interface (“store: false”), which suggests suitable goals. We do not store the sentence you entered and do not evaluate it. To protect against abuse, we briefly count requests per IP address (only in working memory, for at most one hour) and pass on to OpenAI, instead of the IP address, only a pseudonymous identifier formed from it. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (answering your request). The input is not a place for names, addresses or health documents; please describe only the sentence. Only after you choose or describe a goal do we send that goal (at most 160 characters) together with the situation for an answer tailored to it. This flow does not collect an additional anonymous version for a public example library.

Anonymous improvement version. For each AI answer, the AI also rewrites your sentence into a general version without names, places, numbers and other personal details. Our server only allows versions that consist solely of checked everyday words. We store only this version, a counter and the day (without time) in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase, region europe-west1). We do not store the sentence you entered, your IP address or an identifier with it; the version cannot be linked to any person. We use it to improve the Translator, new content on the website and the app. Below the answer you see what was stored, and you can remove it again with one tap. The legal basis, as far as the data is personal data at all, is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (improving our services); you can object at any time, also by the tap below the answer.

If you use the contact form (contact-en.html), we process your name, email address, topic, message and the time in order to reply. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where a contract or subscription is concerned, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (answering enquiries). Please do not write any health data.

Account. Before payment, the website creates an anonymous guest account with Firebase Authentication (Google Ireland Limited). It contains no personal details, only a random ID that carries the purchase. After the purchase you can optionally attach your mobile number to the same account (SMS code via Firebase) or sign in with Apple, so that the subscription also applies in the app. We use your email address only for confirmation, invoice and access. Payment. Payment runs through Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. (Ireland) with Apple Pay, Google Pay or card. Stripe processes your payment data as its own controller for payment processing and for the prevention of money laundering and fraud. We load the Stripe scripts only when you go to pay. We do not receive full card data. We store the Stripe customer ID, email address, account ID, price tier, start and end of the subscription, and codes for the origin of the order (for example channel or campaign). Legal bases: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for invoice and accounting records Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (retention under commercial and tax law, up to ten years).

Subscription in the app. So that your subscription applies in the app, we transmit the account ID and subscription status to Adapty Tech Inc. (see section 7). Situations that you describe in the app or in the Translator do not go to Stripe.

Emails about the contract and weekly email. We send the contract confirmation, receipt and cancellation confirmations and, as long as your subscription runs, the weekly email with the “Moment of the Week” through Brevo. This is part of the booked service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can unsubscribe from the weekly email through the link in every email; your subscription stays unchanged.

Cancellation and withdrawal. For cancellation without logging in, we store your declaration (name, email address, type of cancellation, reason, desired date, time of receipt) as proof (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR, Section 312k BGB (German Civil Code)). We keep the proof until the limitation period for possible claims has expired (as a rule three years from the end of the year); accounting records are kept longer under the statutory periods.

Price reservation. So that a time-limited welcome price really applies to you only until the displayed expiry, our server stores a check value of your email address (an HMAC value formed with a secret key, not the address itself) together with the expiry time in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase, region europe-west1). The check value can only be matched to an address with our key; because this remains possible with additional knowledge, we treat it as personal data. We delete the entry automatically after 30 days. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures at your request).

Resuming the questionnaire after reloading. In the open browser tab, we keep the selected answers as fixed choice codes and the current step (sessionStorage, key demenzCoachQuizProgress.v1). These choices can still concern your personal caregiving situation. This progress entry does not store names, email addresses or free input text. It can be resumed for no more than 30 minutes after the last step; expired entries are discarded on the next access. “Neu anfangen” removes the entry and restarts the questionnaire. This progress entry is not sent to us or anyone else and is not used for measurement or advertising. After reloading, an email address may be needed again to see the result.

Offer, “My Plan” and “Helper sheet”. So that an offer with an expiry time continues in your browser, we store the expiry time and a signature there (browser storage, key demenzCoachAngebot); no personal details are contained in it. For the pages “My Plan” and “Helper sheet”, your browser stores your entries (for example name and chosen moments); they are not sent. This storage is necessary to provide the booked page (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). After a purchase, the page checks with your account at our server whether your subscription applies.

14. How long we store data

  • Content on your device: Until you delete it or remove the app

  • Texts for the AI answer at OpenAI: Not stored for us (store: false); security logs of OpenAI up to 30 days
  • Answers in the working memory of our server: At most 14 days, at the latest until the next restart

  • Usage measurement and crash reports: According to the retention settings at Google (measurement data at most 14 months, crash reports usually 90 days)

  • Price reservation check value: 30 days, then automatic deletion
  • Contact form messages and support emails: Until the request is dealt with and there is no retention duty
