Privacy Choices

Demenz Coach works without a user account. You can optionally use an account for cross-device backup.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Delete local data

  1. Open “Settings” in Demenz Coach.
  2. Choose “Privacy & Data” (“Datenschutz & Daten”).
  3. Confirm “Delete all my data” (“Alle meine Daten löschen”; in older versions “Delete all local data”).

This removes onboarding, learning progress, check-ins, history, consents, care profiles and saved phrases from this device. Deleting the app also removes the local app data.

Account, cloud data and export

Under “Settings → Account and backup” you can sign in with Apple or a phone number, export your cloud data, sign out or choose “Delete account and cloud data”. Before the final deletion you confirm your identity again with Apple or an SMS code. Deleting removes the account and everything backed up with it. It does not end a subscription. Signing out alone deletes nothing.

Withdraw consent to AI help

Under “Settings → Privacy & Data” you switch off “AI answers”. After that no texts or voice recordings go to the AI until you consent again. Withdrawal applies for the future. Without consent there are no AI answers.

Quality sharing

The separate release for OpenAI project logs is off by default and can only be found in the settings. Switching it off prevents new storage; entries already stored expire after 30 days at the latest. For access or earlier deletion, write to the address below.

Measuring usage and crash reports

Measuring usage is off until you choose “Yes, gladly” in the app. You can switch it off at any time under “Settings → Privacy & Data” (“Measure usage”). Technical error diagnostics (crash reports) are on by default and can be switched off there for future reports.

Website: statistics and advertising measurement

The website loads Google Analytics only after you allow “Statistics” in the privacy notice. Via “Privacy settings” at the bottom of every page you can change or withdraw your choice. Withdrawal applies for the future.

Anonymous improvement version in the Demenz Translator

Below every AI answer in the Translator you can see which general version without personal details was stored. One tap removes it again.

Unsubscribe from emails

Every email from us (list, weekly email) contains an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing from the weekly email does not change your subscription.

Withdraw microphone access

In the iOS settings under “Privacy & Security → Microphone” you can switch access off at any time. Text input and local content keep working.

Manage and cancel subscriptions

App Store subscription: with Apple under “Apple ID → Subscriptions”. Web subscription: via “Cancel contracts here” at the bottom of every page (no sign-in needed) or in the customer portal. Deleting the app or data does not end a subscription.

Exercise your privacy rights

For access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection or other questions, write to demenzcoachapp@gmail.com. Please do not send health data. Only you can delete purely local data on your device, because we have no access to it. More in the Privacy Policy.
