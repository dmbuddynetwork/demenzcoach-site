Clear limits

Communication support. Not treatment.

Dementia Coach is intended for adults and provides general communication, orientation, and learning support only.

No medical service

No diagnosis, therapy, symptom monitoring, medication recommendation, individual care planning, or other professional decision.

Suggestions may be wrong

AI-generated and prepared content may be incomplete, unsuitable, or outdated. Users must review it themselves.

Not for emergencies

In immediate danger or a medical emergency, call 911 in the United States or your local emergency service.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Important safety and privacy choices at a glance.

Does the app replace medical or nursing advice?

No. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend medication or treatment, or create an individual care plan. Contact qualified professionals for clinical questions.

Are personalized responses created by AI?

They may be. After explicit consent, OpenAI can generate personalized suggestions. They are labeled as AI suggestions, may contain errors, and must be reviewed. Prepared situations work without this consent.

What information may I enter?

Describe situations anonymously. Do not enter names, addresses, contact details, birth dates, insurance numbers, or detailed patient records.

Is the app intended for emergencies?

No. The app does not monitor anyone or alert emergency services. Use local emergency services immediately when someone may be in danger.
