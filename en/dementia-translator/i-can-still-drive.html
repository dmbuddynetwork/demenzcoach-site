You hear:

“I can still drive” – talking about driving with someone with dementia

What's behind it

The car stands for independence and pride. Giving it up can feel like a big loss.

What you can say

“You've always been a good driver. I know this is hard.”

“I'll drive today, and you tell me the way.”

“Let's look together at how you can still get everywhere.”

Are they crying or very upset? Then just say: “I'm with you.” The sentences above help once things are a little calmer.

These are suggestions, not rules. Adjust the words and watch how they land. Add your names in the translator

What you can do

Ask your doctor or your local driving authority how fitness to drive is checked.

Didn't help? Go to the second try ↓

Said in other ways: “Give me the car keys.”, “I've been driving for 50 years.”

If that doesn't help

“Let's leave the car today. I'll take you.”

Change the subject for now and offer a ride or a walk instead. Come back to it another day.

It's okay to stop. If it gets louder or more heated: stop talking, stop asking for anything, step back if it's safe, and start again later. If anyone is in danger: call 911 (US) or 999 (UK).

More about this

Background

You can take the feeling seriously, and let others, like the doctor, share the responsibility for the decision.

Every moment is different. Often it's one of these:

  • Fear of losing independence.
  • Pride in decades of driving.
  • Not noticing that driving has become unsafe.
  • Worry about getting around without a car.

Better to avoid

Things that often don't help:

  • arguing about mistakes they made while driving
  • taking the keys without a word
  • making fun of it

What can help next

  • Plan other ways to get around: family, neighbors, taxis.
  • Keep the keys out of sight if driving is no longer safe.

When talking isn't enough

In danger, after a fall, with breathing problems or unconsciousness, or if you're unsure: call 911 (US) or 999 (UK). For medical advice, contact your doctor. This tool can't judge how urgent something is.
