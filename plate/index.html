Small trick, big difference

5 everyday tricks

You can read two tricks right here. Get all five free as a list.

1A red plate

With dementia, light food on a white plate often blends in. On red, it stands out. And: don’t rush. Sit down and eat together.

Study: about a quarter more food eaten (Boston University, 2004)

2A red cup

A brightly colored cup, placed where it’s easy to see, gets picked up much more often than a clear glass.

Study: almost twice as much to drink (same study)

3The door trick

So Mom can find her room again.

Study: room found more than twice as often

🔒 How it works is in the list. Get it free

3An old photo on the door

A large photo of them as a young adult, plus their name, on the bedroom door. Many recognize their younger face more easily.

Small study: room found far more often, 85% instead of 34%

4The song trick

When washing and dressing turn into a fight every morning.

Study from Sweden: much less resistance

🔒 How it works is in the list. Get it free

4A song while dressing

Sing a familiar old song while helping with washing and dressing. Often they hum along.

Study from Sweden: much less resistance, more joy

5The light trick

When Dad gets up restless at night.

Study with 64 seniors: less fear of falling

🔒 How it works is in the list. Get it free

5A motion-sensor night light

A floor light from the bed to the bathroom that turns on by itself. No more searching for the switch in the dark.

Study with 64 seniors: less fear of falling, better sleep

Who’s behind this

Hi, I’m Debby

I’m a geriatric nurse from Germany. I’ve worked in geriatric psychiatry, palliative care and dementia care. There I learned to meet people with dementia calmly and as equals, especially when things get hard.

I also know dementia from my own family: my grandmother had Alzheimer’s. I know how different it feels when it’s someone you love.

That’s why I founded Dementia Coach. Here I collect small tricks that really help in everyday life, written so you can try them right away.
